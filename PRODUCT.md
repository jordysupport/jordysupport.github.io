# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People learning to use AI agents for research, writing, meeting notes, and everyday work. Small businesses can also ask for personal help by email.

## Product Purpose

Jordy Support is a learning site first. It provides free guides, worked examples, downloadable playbooks, and software. Visitors should be able to choose a task, try it, and judge the result.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The existing MkDocs Material site and GitHub Pages deployment remain in use. All current page and download URLs are permanent. Guides and downloads stay accessible without payment or signup. Newsletter signup is optional. Personal services use jordy@jordysupport.com, with scope and pricing discussed privately. Publish changes through the existing GitHub Pages workflow after Jordy's approval.

## Brand Commitments

Use the Jordy Support name and we/us voice. Write naturally and concretely. No marketing em dashes, invented testimonials, guarantees, credentials, or outcomes. Agentic AI is the main subject; technology advice, web design, hosting, and SEO remain optional services. Keep TUSK as a separate software download rather than turning learning pages into a promotion.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing guides and four playbook ZIPs, public software repositories and release notes, TUSK screenshots under docs/assets/tusk, and VALE capture media under docs/assets/vale-demo. No verified customer testimonials or service results were supplied.

## Product Principles

- Teach through a concrete task and a result the reader can check.
- Explain the limits of prompts, permissions, memory, and sources accurately.
- Preserve free access and existing links.
- Keep personal services optional.
