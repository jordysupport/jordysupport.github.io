# AI & Technology Services

We help small businesses make technology work for them.

We focus on agentic AI: setting up AI that can use tools and work through useful tasks, with you in control.

</div>

## How we can help

- **AI agents and automation.** Build workflows for research, documents, and repetitive tasks using software that fits how you work.
- **Technology advice and consultation.** Talk through your options or get help with something that isn't working.
- **Web design, hosting, and SEO.** Build or redesign your site, get it online, and help people find it in search.
- **Website copy and content cleanup.** Rewrite confusing pages, tidy up outdated information, and explain what your business does.
- **Ongoing website and technical support.** Keep your site updated and get help when technical problems come up.
